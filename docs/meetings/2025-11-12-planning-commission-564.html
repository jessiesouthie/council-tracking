Revocable permits for alternative animal plans

The commission recommended the council adopt rules on alternative animal management plans and other zoning provisions, with the commission, not staff, able to revoke a plan.

What it means: If you keep animals under an alternative plan, the commission would decide whether you lose it, not the planning director or code enforcement officer. Nothing changes until the council votes.

passed with a 4-1 vote.

  • Brent StrongNo
  • Bryan FreeYes
  • Craig WhitingYes
  • Jason AllenYes
  • Robert FoxExcused
  • Rod HessYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Whiting moved to recommend approval to the City Council of Item 7.B., Alternative Animal Management Plans and Other Zoning Provisions, with the change that the Planning Commission is the revocation authority instead of the planning director or the code enforcement officer. Commissioner Free seconded the motion.
