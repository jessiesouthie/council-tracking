ORDINANCE - An Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Uøh, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code, Chapter 17.80.080 Off-premises Ladder Signs. Economic Development Director Aaron Sanborn explained the ladder signs in the City were previously managed by the Lehi Area Chamber of Commerce. The program is now being administered by the City until the Eagle Mountain City Chamber of Commerce begins management. He said the current Municipal Code is sparse in details regarding program administration. This updated Code will set a priority list for entities advertising on the ladder signs, giving priority to local commercial and home-based businesses. It also adds language to create an application process. Approved maps specify locations where signs can be added. The sign locations are at least 1,000 feet apart, except in specific locations with high traffic and commercial uses. Signs that pose traffic hazards will be moved. New signs will only be added when all existing sign space is in use. Mr. Sanborn said items for the Council to consider include a potential limitation to the number of signs an entity can lease, a process in which an entity can use to install a sign in a location not on the approved sign location maps, and where the applications will be submitted. Applications can be submitted to the Economic Development Director, or to the Eagle Mountain Chamber of Commerce, if an agreement is in place. Councilmember Reaves asked how staff will determine what signs need to be moved for safety reasons. Mr. Sanborn said there is only one sign that poses a safety concern; this is located at Pony Express Parkway and Porters Crossing. He said it is too close to the intersection and will be moved" A standard can be in place to determine how far signs must be from intersections and from residences. Councilmember Clark asked why a sign location was included on Porters Crossing, far south of Pony Express Parkway. Mr. Sanborn explained that is a commercial area. Mayor lMestmoreland opened the public hearing at 7:40 p.m. As there were no comments, he closed the public hearing. Eagle Mountain City Council Meeting - June 18,2019 Page l0 of14
