Gateway Park Phase 3 lots, street renamed

The commission recommended the early layout of lots for Gateway Park Phase 3 and asked that Gateway Park Boulevard be renamed Wild Blossom Boulevard.

What it means: If the council agrees, homes in this phase could face directly onto the area's larger through streets unless the developer finds room for internal roads. Anyone giving directions will need the new street name.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brent StrongYes
  • Bryan FreeYes
  • Chad DeCourseyYes
  • Jason AllenYes
  • Laura JensenExcused
  • Rod HessYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Hess moved to recommend approval to the City Council of item 7.B., Preliminary Plat – Gateway Park Phase 3, with the following conditions: 1. Unless the developer can find a reasonable way to construct internal streets on the proposed lots, that the lots be permitted to front onto the proposed arterial and collector streets. 2. Gateway Park Boulevard be renamed Wild Blossom Boulevard. Commissioner Allen seconded the motion.

Spring Run Plaza Lot 2 building plan

The commission recommended the plan for Lot 2 of Spring Run Plaza, on condition that at least 60% of the east wall is faced in high-quality natural material.

What it means: The council has the final vote. If it agrees, the building's east side will be mostly natural material, and the owners must file a shared parking and upkeep agreement with city staff.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brent StrongYes
  • Bryan FreeYes

  • Laura JensenExcused
  • Rod HessYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Strong moved to recommend approval to the City Council of Item 7.D., Site Plan Spring Run Plaza Lot 2 with the following conditions: 1. The East elevation shall be clad with at least 60% high-quality natural material. 2. Shared parking and maintenance agreement to be filled with staff as part of the site plan application. Commissioner Hess seconded the motion.
