Tax increment plan for 80 acres at Pole Canyon

The agency adopted the official plan for the Pole Canyon project area, 80 acres of industrial-planned land where tax increment can pay for development.

What it means: The site sits in the Pole Canyon master plan and is aimed at industry, with a food manufacturing business under discussion. A resident who spoke raised traffic and the two roads planned there.

Outcome not recorded

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

Board Member Clark moved to approve a resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, Utah adopting an Official Project Area Plan for the Pole Canyon Community Reinvestment Project Area. Board Member Curtis seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Colby Curtis, Stephanie Gricius, Benjamin Reaves, Donna Burnham, and Melissa Clark The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Pole Canyon project budget adopted

The agency adopted the spending plan for the Pole Canyon project area, setting where its tax increment revenue comes from and what it may be spent on.

What it means: This is the money side of the Pole Canyon deal, with a cost-benefit analysis putting the city's benefit at about $3 million. A tenth of the increment is set aside for affordable housing.

Draft 2019-20 agency budget and May 21 hearing

The agency board adopted a tentative budget for the 2019-2020 year and set a May 21, 2019 public hearing before the final budget is adopted.

What it means: Nothing is locked in at this stage. If you wanted a say on the agency's spending, May 21 was the date to speak.

City signs its own Pole Canyon tax agreement

The agency and Eagle Mountain City agreed on how the city will collect and remit the property tax growth from land inside the Pole Canyon project area.

What it means: Both sides of this deal are the same five people, sitting once as the council and once as the agency board. Your city tax bill is untouched; the city's share of new Pole Canyon revenue is what moves.

Utah County signs Pole Canyon tax agreement

The agency board approved the Utah County version of the Pole Canyon agreement, covering collection and remittance of property tax growth from the project area.

What it means: County services carry on as before, and no household pays anything new. This is the county's signature on the tax sharing that funds the Pole Canyon project.

School district signs Pole Canyon tax agreement

The agency approved an agreement with Alpine School District for collecting and remitting the property tax growth generated inside the Pole Canyon project area.

The agency board signed an agreement with the Unified Fire Service Area to collect and pass on property tax growth from the Pole Canyon project area.

What it means: Fire coverage where you live is not altered by this. The agreement sets the route by which the fire district's share of new Pole Canyon tax revenue reaches the agency.
