City Council Regular Session

Eagle Mountain City Council, 5 July 2019

4:00 PM

The city published no minutes for this meeting. What follows is the agenda it posted beforehand, so it says what the council intended to take up — not what was decided, and not who voted for it. If minutes are approved later, they will replace this page.

1. City Administrator Information Items

This is an opportunity for the City Administrator to provide information to the City Council. These items are for information only and do not require action by the City Council.
