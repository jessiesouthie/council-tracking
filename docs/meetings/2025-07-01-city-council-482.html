Eagle Mountain City Council, 1 July 2025

7 motions · 7 ordinance lines · 7 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Transportation Master Plan put on hold

The council delayed adopting the Transportation Master Plan, the city's long-range plan for its road network.

What it means: No road decisions are locked in by this. The plan shapes where future roads and improvements go, and it has to come back to the council before it takes effect.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to table a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Adopting the Transportation Master Plan. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Parking rule changes rejected

The council moved to reject proposed changes to the city's parking rules.

What it means: The parking rules stay exactly as they are today. There is nothing new you need to do, and no new restrictions on where you park.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayNo
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to deny an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 10.10 Parking. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Consent agenda approved

The council approved its consent agenda, the bundle of items passed together in one vote; the record does not list what was in it.

What it means: Nothing in the record points to a specific change for residents, because the items grouped into this single vote were not listed.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Sale of 0.156-acre city parcel postponed

The council held off on selling a 0.156-acre piece of city-owned land, leaving the terms of the sale undecided.

What it means: The city keeps the parcel for now. Unless you live next to it, this one passes you by.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Bypass Road land sale put on hold

The council delayed a decision on selling up to 20.35 acres of city-owned land known as the Bypass Road.

What it means: The city keeps the 20.35 acres for now. Any sale would have to come back to the council before it could go through.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to table a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Disposal of up to 20.35 acres of Public Real Property known as the Bypass Road. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Sign rules rewrite delayed

The council shelved proposed changes to the city's sign rules and permit requirements without voting on them.

What it means: The existing sign rules stay in force, so businesses and homeowners follow the same permit requirements as before until the council takes the item back up.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to table an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 17.80 Sign Regulations and Sign Permits. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Zayo fiber franchise deal put on hold

The council set aside a franchise agreement that would let Zayo Group run fiber lines through Eagle Mountain right-of-way, with no decision made.

What it means: Nothing changes for you today. The agreement comes back to the council before Zayo gets permission to build in city streets.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes

  • Melissa ClarkYes

Councilmember Burnham moved to table an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah approving a Franchise Agreement with Zayo Group, LLC. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
