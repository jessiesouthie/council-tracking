Eagle Mountain City Council, 1 July 2025

7 motions · 7 ordinance lines · 7 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Transportation Master Plan put on hold

The council delayed adopting the Transportation Master Plan, the city's long-range plan for its road network.

What it means: No road decisions are locked in by this. The plan shapes where future roads and improvements go, and it has to come back to the council before it takes effect.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to table a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Adopting the Transportation Master Plan. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Parking rule changes rejected

The council moved to reject proposed changes to the city's parking rules.

What it means: The parking rules stay exactly as they are today. There is nothing new you need to do, and no new restrictions on where you park.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayNo
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to deny an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 10.10 Parking. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.
