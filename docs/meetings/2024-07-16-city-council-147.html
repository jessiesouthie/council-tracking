Eagle Mountain City Council, 16 July 2024

4 motions · 12 ordinance lines · 6 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Consent agenda approved

The council approved its consent agenda, pulling the WDG website change order out of the batch for separate discussion.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this is the routine step that passes uncontested items in a single vote.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to approve the Consent Agenda, moving item 9.A. WDG Website Services Change Order #1 to the Scheduled Items for further discussion. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

City website contract change order approved

The council approved the first change order on its website contract with WDG, after rewriting a clause about the vendor's budget, timeline, and fees.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this is contract paperwork for the company building the city's website. The reworded clause lets the vendor adjust the final cost or schedule if unknown variables come up.

passed with a vote of 4:1.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to approve the WDG Website Services Change Order #1 with the following change: Everything after the first sentence in Section 2 will be removed and replaced with, “While this estimate is prepared with precision and with the details provided to WDG during the estimation, WDG has the right to account for unknown variables that may affect the final budget, timeline, or fee structure.” Councilmember Clark seconded the motion. The recording of the discussion regarding the motion can be found online here at 01:51:40.

Parking rules changed for hotels and motels

The city revised the parking table in its zoning code, changing how many parking spaces hotels, motels, and bed-and-breakfasts must provide.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day for most residents. This affects anyone building or running a hotel, motel, or bed-and-breakfast in Eagle Mountain, who now works from a different parking requirement.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to approve an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 17.55.120 Tables, Regarding Parking Requirements for Hotel, Motel, and Breakfast Uses. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

New rules for second kitchens in homes

The city added a new section to its code, Chapter 17.69, setting rules for second kitchens and kitchenettes in homes.

What it means: If you have or are planning a second kitchen in your house, it is now regulated by the city. The motion itself doesn't spell out the requirements, so check with the planning department before building one.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to approve An Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending Eagle Mountain Municipal Code to Enact Chapter 17.69 Second Kitchens or Kitchenettes. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.
