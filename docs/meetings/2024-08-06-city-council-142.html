Eagle Mountain City Council, 6 August 2024

3 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 7 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Joshua Biagio joins the Library Advisory Board

The council appointed Joshua Biagio to the Library Advisory Board to finish out a term that runs through December 2025.

What it means: The board advises on library matters and now has a full bench. Nothing changes for you at the library counter.

passed with a unanimous vote.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to appoint Joshua Biagio to the Library Advisory Board, completing a term through December 2025. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Consent agenda approved

The council passed its consent agenda as a single batch; the record does not list which items were included.

What it means: Consent items are the routine business the council approves together without separate debate. The record here doesn't say what was in this batch.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes

  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

New school district proposal approved

The council approved the proposal to create a new school district and set the date for its final decision on it.

What it means: This advances the proposal rather than settling it; a final council vote is still to come. If a new district is created, it would change which school district Eagle Mountain belongs to.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving an Amendment to the Adobe Value Incentive Plan Terms and Conditions. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.
