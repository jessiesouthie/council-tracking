Joshua Biagio appointed to Library Advisory Board

The council appointed Joshua Biagio to the Library Advisory Board, finishing a term that runs through December 2025.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day — this fills an unexpired seat on a board that advises the city on the library.

New school district proposal approved

The council approved the proposal to create a new school district and set the date for its final decision on it.

What it means: This advances the proposal rather than settling it — a final council vote is still to come. If a new district is created, it would change which school district Eagle Mountain belongs to.
