Steeplechase South subdivision plat amended

The city approved a second plat amendment for the Steeplechase South subdivision at 877 N Pony Express Parkway.

What it means: This adjusts the recorded layout of one subdivision on Pony Express Parkway. Unless you live in or are buying in Steeplechase South, nothing changes for you.

Update street cross-section diagrams in city code

The city updated the right-of-way profile illustrations in its code — the standard diagrams showing how streets are to be laid out.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day. These are the technical drawings engineers and developers follow when designing new streets.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.35.140 for Right of Way Profile Illustrations. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

New rules for fences and privacy screening

The city amended its code covering fences, privacy screening, and the table of land uses allowed in each zone; the motion does not state what specifically changed.

What it means: If you are planning a fence or a screening wall on your property, the rules you have to build to have changed — check the current code before you start.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.35.090 Privacy Screening, Section 17.40.020 Land Use Table, and Section 17.60.120 General Fencing Provisions. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Change the sign-off process for change orders

The city rewrote its purchasing rules to change how change orders — the cost adjustments made after a contract is already underway — get approved.

What it means: This is internal paperwork and nothing changes for residents. It affects how city staff and the council sign off on added costs for work already under contract.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Purchasing Policy Section 3.20.030 (H) to Modify the Approval Process for Change Orders. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Traffic calming added to priority list

The council added traffic calming and other traffic-related actions to the city's list of legislative priorities.

What it means: No speed bumps or new signs come from this on its own. It flags traffic as something the city wants to work on; any real change comes later.

passed with a unanimous vote.

The motion as the minutes record it

Youth court and justice court added to priority list

The council added a youth court, a justice court, and other public safety measures to the city's list of legislative priorities.

What it means: Nothing changes yet. This only puts these ideas on the city's priority list — an actual youth or justice court would take separate decisions later.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
