All meetings / 3 June 2025

Eagle Mountain City Council, 3 June 2025

7 motions · 16 ordinance lines · 2 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Consent agenda approved

The council approved its consent agenda, the batch of items passed together without separate discussion; the motion does not list what was included.

What it means: Nothing here changes for residents on its own. The individual items are listed on the meeting agenda.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Redraw lot lines at Steeplechase South

The council approved a second plat amendment for the Steeplechase South subdivision at 877 N Pony Express Parkway, adjusting the recorded subdivision map.

What it means: This touches property boundaries inside one subdivision on Pony Express Parkway. If you do not own or plan to build on land there, it passes you by.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Steeplechase South Subdivision Second Plat Amendment (877 N Pony Express Parkway). Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Update road right-of-way drawings in city code

The council amended the city's code section covering the illustrations that show how streets and the land beside them must be laid out.

What it means: Engineers and developers drawing up new streets work from these diagrams. For everyone else it is a technical drawing swap with nothing to see.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.35.140 for Right of Way Profile Illustrations. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

New rules for fences and privacy screening

The city amended its code covering fences, privacy screening, and the table of land uses allowed in each zone; the motion does not state what specifically changed.

What it means: If you are planning a fence or a screening wall on your property, the rules you have to build to have changed, so check the current code before you start.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.35.090 Privacy Screening, Section 17.40.020 Land Use Table, and Section 17.60.120 General Fencing Provisions. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Change how contract change orders get approved

The council rewrote the purchasing rules to change who signs off when the cost or scope of a city contract changes mid-project.

What it means: An internal spending control that lives inside city hall. You will not run into it unless you hold a contract with the city.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkExcused
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Purchasing Policy Section 3.20.030 (H) to Modify the Approval Process for Change Orders. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Traffic calming added to priority list

The council added traffic calming and other traffic-related actions to the city's list of legislative priorities.

What it means: No speed bumps or new signs come from this on its own. It flags traffic as something the city wants to work on; any real change comes later.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to add traffic calming measures and traffic related actions to the Legislative Priorities List. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Push for a youth court and justice court

The council added a youth court, a justice court and other public safety measures to the city's legislative priorities list, to be discussed with public safety partners and the Public Safety Director.

What it means: Nothing changes yet. This puts the idea on the city's wish list for talks with its public safety partners; any actual court would need separate decisions later.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to add discussions with public safety partners and the Public Safety Director to include a Youth Court, Justice Court and other public safety measures to the Legislative Priorities List. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
