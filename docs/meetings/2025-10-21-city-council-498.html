Backyard animal rules sent back for more work

The council sent proposed rules on alternative animal management plans, and the related zoning changes, back to the Planning Commission for further review.

What it means: Nothing changes for you yet. If you keep animals on your property, or want to, the rules stay as they are until the Planning Commission finishes its review and the council takes it up again.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes

  • Melissa ClarkYes

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to remand the Ordinance Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Title 6 and Title 17 to Establish Standards for Alternative Animal Management Plans and Update Related Zoning Provisions back to the Planning Commission to review additional changes. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.
