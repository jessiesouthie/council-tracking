Rec center funding pulled, $1M pooled for parks

The council reworked how proceeds from the utility sale are spent, moving $2.6 million earmarked for a recreation center into unallocated funds and pooling $1 million for parks.

What it means: A recreation center is off the funding list for now — council members said it could be revisited later, possibly starting with buying land. The $1 million park fund still covers Silver Lake amphitheater, bike park, and Nolan Park improvements.
