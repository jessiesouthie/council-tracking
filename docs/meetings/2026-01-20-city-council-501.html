New sign rules for data center projects

The city rewrote the part of its sign code covering directional signs for data centers and other large-scale developments, adopting it with edits presented that night.

What it means: The companies behind big projects and the staff reviewing their sign applications work from a different rule now. Homeowners are not asked to do anything.

Hillside building rules held back

The council delayed a set of changes to the city's slope development rules, which govern building on steep ground.

What it means: Builders working hillside lots keep planning under the rules already on the books. If you are not developing on a slope, this one passes you by.

Rewrite of city government rules put on hold

The council shelved a rewrite of Title 2, the chapter of city code that sets out how Eagle Mountain governs itself.

What it means: The current rules on how the city runs stay in force. Nothing on your street or your bill changes, and the record does not say when the rewrite returns.
