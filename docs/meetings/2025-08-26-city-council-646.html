Eagle Mountain City Council, 26 August 2025

1 motion · 0 ordinance lines · 1 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Primary results certified; November ballot set

The city certified the August 12 primary, in which 6,866 of 26,930 mailed ballots came back, and named the six candidates advancing to the November 4 election.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day — this makes the primary official. Your November 4 ballot will list Melissa Clark and Jared Gray for mayor, and Emily Buss, Brett Wright, Craig Whiting, and Charlotte Ducos for council.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved that the Board of Canvassers verify the results of the 2025 Municipal Primary Election by ratifying the results of the canvass; accept the report of the Election Official and certify the election results; and declare Four-year Mayoral candidates Melissa Clark and Jared R. Gray; and Four-year City Council candidates Emily Buss, Brett L Wright, Craig Whiting, and Charlotte Ducos eligible to be on the ballot and nominated for the General Municipal Election on November 4, 2025. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
