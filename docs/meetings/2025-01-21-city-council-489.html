Jacob Adams appointed to Youth Council

The council appointed Jacob Adams to the Youth Council for a term running through June 30, 2025.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this fills a seat on the city's youth advisory body.

Two items pulled for separate discussion

The council approved its consent agenda but pulled the civic campus land purchase and the lobbying contract out for individual discussion.

What it means: This is procedural housekeeping — it only means two items got their own debate instead of passing in a batch.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to approve the consent agenda, removing Items 11.C. and 11.E. from the consent agenda and placing them at the end of the scheduled items for further discussion. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Energy code changes put on hold

The council delayed a decision on changes to the city's energy-related building and land use rules in Title 17.

What it means: Nothing changes for now — the existing rules stay in place until the council brings the item back.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to table an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Title 17 Energy-Related Code Amendments. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Buy 21 acres for a future civic campus

The council took up an agreement with Ivory Homes and SITLA to buy 20.99 acres for a future civic campus, with limits on commercial building size struck from the deal.

What it means: If the purchase goes through, the city would own land it intends to use for future public buildings. Nothing changes for you now — no buildings, timeline, or price are set in this item.

Outcome not recorded

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Purchase Agreement with Ivory Homes and SITLA to purchase 20.99 acres of Land for a Future Civic Campus with the following changes: 1. Amending references to Fionnuala Kofoed, City Recorder to the new City Recorder, Gina Olsen 2. Strike any restriction/language on commercial size restrictions. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion. Discussion took place regarding the size restrictions, drive-throughs/deed restrictions, and what would be considered a “Public Works” facility.

City hires a lobbyist for $60,000 a year

The city signed a one-year, $60,000 contract for lobbying services to represent Eagle Mountain's interests.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day — this is the city paying an outside firm to advocate for it, most likely at the state level.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving an Agreement to Provide Lobbying Services for one year at a rate of $60,000 annually. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.
