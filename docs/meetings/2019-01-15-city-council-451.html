Councilmember Clark moved to approve the Limitless Event agreement between CBRS, LLC and Eagle Mountain City. Councilmember Reaves seconded the motion. AMENDED MOTION: Councilmember Clark moved to approve the Limitless Event agreement between CBRS, LLC and Eagle Mountain City, with the amendment that the City may offer law enforcement and fire services. Councilmember Reaves seconded the motion. Mr. Shelton asked that the contract include a time limit for notification if the City does not elect to provide law enforcement and fire services. Mr. Cook stated they could add a six-month notification requirement in the agreement. Mr. Shelton explained a clause in the agreement allows either side to terminate based on hardship, with a nine-month notice., and that clause could be utilized for notification. Mr. Cook clarified that if the Council wishes to have the City provide safety services, the agreement needs to state the City will provide law enforcement and fire services, as long as it does not create a hard cost to the City.
