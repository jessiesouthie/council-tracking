Rules changed for building into yard setbacks

The council amended the city code rule governing how far a structure may project into a required yard setback.

What it means: If you are planning to build near your property line, the limits on how far a structure can extend into the setback have changed. The motion itself does not state the new limits — check with the city first.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes

Expired development deals now trigger rezoning

The council required the planning director to start rezoning land whenever a master development agreement or development agreement on it expires or is terminated.

What it means: If you own land covered by a development agreement that lapses, the city will move to rezone it rather than leave the old zoning in place. Most residents will not notice this.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.10.080 Criteria for Approval of MDAs and changing section F to read “Expired or otherwise terminated agreements upon expiration or termination of a Master Development Agreement or a Development Agreement that zoned property, the Planning Director shall initiate proceedings to rezone the land.” Also, enacting Section 17.90.090 Rezone Initiation Post Development Agreement Expiration or Termination with the following change in 17.90.090 to read “Expired or otherwise terminated agreements upon expiration or termination of a Master Development Agreement or a Development Agreement that zoned property, the Planning Director shall initiate proceedings to rezone the land.” Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Setback measuring change delayed

The council put off a change to how the city measures setbacks on site plans, measuring from the existing right-of-way instead of the curb.

What it means: Nothing changes for now. The current measuring rules stay in place for anyone submitting a site plan until the council takes the item up again.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to table an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 17.100.050 Site Plan Development Standards clarifying the method of measuring and also clarifying from the distance of existing right-of-way, not existing curb, and a diagram to give direction to that effect. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Outside brokers allowed for big city land deals

The city authorized staff to hire a real estate brokerage or a real estate attorney when buying or selling major city property.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day — this only sets who the city can bring in to handle its larger land purchases and sales.

passed 4:1.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing Staff to Engage the Services of a Third-Party Real Estate Brokerage or Real Estate Attorney for Major Land Acquisition and Disposal of Significant Real Property in accordance with the changes stated. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Council board assignments put on hold

The council kept its 2024 board liaison assignments until the mayor can meet with members to set new ones, and asked staff for a list of all boards.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this is internal housekeeping about which councilmember sits in on which city board and commission.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to table the Assignment of City Council Board Liaisons position and continue with our 2024 assignments until such time that the Mayor can meet with councilmembers to determine the new assignments. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion. Councilmember Wood requested that the City Manager prepare a list of all potential boards and commissions for City Council review. AMENDED MOTION: Councilmember Clark moved to table the Assignment of City Council Board Liaisons position and continue with our 2024 assignments until such time that the Mayor can meet with councilmembers to determine the new assignments and request our City Manager meet with the Mayor and prepare a list for City Council of all potential boards and commissions that the City wants a liaison. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.
