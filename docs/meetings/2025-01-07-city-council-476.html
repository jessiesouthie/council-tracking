Eagle Mountain City Council, 7 January 2025

11 motions · 21 ordinance lines · 8 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Brett Wright named mayor pro tem for 2025

Councilmember Brett Wright was chosen as mayor pro tempore for 2025.

What it means: Wright runs council meetings when the mayor cannot attend. It decides who holds the gavel on a given night and nothing more.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood nominates Councilmember Brett Wright as Mayor Pro Tempore for 2025. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Adam Jacobs joins the library board

Adam Jacobs was appointed to the Library Advisory Board for three years, through 2027.

What it means: A second seat on the same volunteer board was filled the same night. Library hours, services and fees are untouched.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Gina Olsen named city recorder

The council adopted its consent agenda with two changes from the work session: revisions to the fiscal year financial audit and the appointment of Gina Olsen as city recorder.

What it means: Olsen now keeps the city's official records and handles public records requests, so hers is the office you deal with when you ask for a document. The audit change is bookkeeping.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to adopt the Consent Agenda. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion. Councilmembers clarified the changes to the Consent Agenda that were made during the Work Session in relation to the fiscal year financial audit. AMENDED MOTION: Councilmember Clark moved to adopt the Consent Agenda with the changes as discussed in Work Session to the audit. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion. Councilmembers clarified changes to the Consent Agenda to include the appointment of City Recorder Gina Olsen. AMENDED MOTION: Councilmember Clark moved to adopt the Consent Agenda with the changes as discussed in Work Session to the audit and appointing Gina Olsen as Eagle Mountain City Recorder. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Cedar Corners land use map change delayed

A change to the general plan's future land use map covering Cedar Corners Phase 5 was delayed, with the public hearing continued.

What it means: This is the companion piece to the zoning item on the same ground, and it waits with it. Neighbors keep their chance to comment at a later meeting.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Cedar Corners Phase 5 zoning change put off

The council delayed a zoning change for the land known as Cedar Corners Phase 5 and left the public hearing open.

What it means: If you live near Cedar Corners, you still have a chance to speak: the hearing was continued rather than closed. The zoning on that ground is unchanged for now.

Rules changed for building into yard setbacks

The council amended the city code rule governing how far a structure may project into a required yard setback.

What it means: If you are planning to build near your property line, the limits on how far a structure can extend into the setback have changed. The motion itself does not state the new limits, so check with the city first.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 17.25.060 Projection into Setbacks. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Expired development deals now trigger rezoning

The council required the planning director to start rezoning land whenever a master development agreement or development agreement on it expires or is terminated.

What it means: If you own land covered by a development agreement that lapses, the city will move to rezone it rather than leave the old zoning in place. Most residents will not notice this.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 16.10.080 Criteria for Approval of MDAs and changing section F to read “Expired or otherwise terminated agreements upon expiration or termination of a Master Development Agreement or a Development Agreement that zoned property, the Planning Director shall initiate proceedings to rezone the land.” Also, enacting Section 17.90.090 Rezone Initiation Post Development Agreement Expiration or Termination with the following change in 17.90.090 to read “Expired or otherwise terminated agreements upon expiration or termination of a Master Development Agreement or a Development Agreement that zoned property, the Planning Director shall initiate proceedings to rezone the land.” Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Setback measuring rules left unsettled

The council delayed a code change that would measure site plan distances from the existing right-of-way rather than the existing curb, with a diagram added for clarity.

What it means: Nothing changes for now. When it returns, it will matter to anyone drawing up a site plan for a commercial or residential project, and to nobody else.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to table an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 17.100.050 Site Plan Development Standards clarifying the method of measuring and also clarifying from the distance of existing right-of-way, not existing curb, and a diagram to give direction to that effect. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

City can hire brokers to buy and sell land

Staff were authorized to hire an outside real estate brokerage or attorney when the city buys or sells significant property.

What it means: Big city land deals will now run through a paid outside broker or lawyer rather than staff alone. Residents see nothing directly, though it shapes how the city handles the property it owns.

passed 4:1.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing Staff to Engage the Services of a Third-Party Real Estate Brokerage or Real Estate Attorney for Major Land Acquisition and Disposal of Significant Real Property in accordance with the changes stated. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Board liaison assignments put off

The council kept its 2024 board liaison assignments in place until the mayor can meet with members to set new ones, and asked the city manager for a list of every board that needs a liaison.

What it means: Which councilmember sits in on which board stays as it was last year. Nothing here changes a city service or a bill.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to table the Assignment of City Council Board Liaisons position and continue with our 2024 assignments until such time that the Mayor can meet with councilmembers to determine the new assignments. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion. Councilmember Wood requested that the City Manager prepare a list of all potential boards and commissions for City Council review. AMENDED MOTION: Councilmember Clark moved to table the Assignment of City Council Board Liaisons position and continue with our 2024 assignments until such time that the Mayor can meet with councilmembers to determine the new assignments and request our City Manager meet with the Mayor and prepare a list for City Council of all potential boards and commissions that the City wants a liaison. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.
