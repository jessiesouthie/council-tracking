Special Session — Joint Work Session with the Planning Commission

Eagle Mountain City Council, 30 October 2024

0 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 0 resolution lines

Summary

Joint work session with the Planning Commission. Planning Director Brandon Larson introduced consultants from GSBS Architecture for a discussion on the General Plan Future Land Use Map (FLUM), separating land-use and transportation maps, MDA coverage (~60%), open-space categories, and walkable-community planning. Elizabeth Fewkes presented the General Plan outline, and MHTN Architects presented updates to the Downtown Area Plan.
