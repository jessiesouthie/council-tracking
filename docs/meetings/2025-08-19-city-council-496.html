All meetings / 19 August 2025

Eagle Mountain City Council, 19 August 2025

13 motions · 16 ordinance lines · 18 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Susan Allen joins Senior Advisory Board

The council appointed Susan Allen to the Senior Advisory Board for a term ending December 31, 2025.

What it means: Residents who use the city's senior programs gain one more voice advising on them. Otherwise this is a seat being filled.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to approve the appointment of Susan Allen to the Senior Advisory Board through December 31, 2025. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Two items pulled off the consent list

The council passed its consent agenda after pulling items 13.A. and 13.C. out for open discussion.

What it means: The effect was to give two items a real airing instead of a bundled vote. Nothing lands on residents here.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to adopt the Consent Agenda removing items 13.A. and 13.C. to be placed on the Scheduled Items for further discussion. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Transportation Master Plan adopted

The council adopted the city's Transportation Master Plan, the blueprint for which roads get built and widened as Eagle Mountain grows.

What it means: The plan itself does not break ground or spend money, but it decides which routes the city plans around. If a future road runs near your street, this document is where it first appears.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Adopting the Transportation Master Plan. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Discount on a rezone fee turned down

The council declined Steve McCleery's request to cut the fee for his rezone application from $1,350 to $760.

What it means: Only the applicant is affected. He pays the full $1,350. The city's rezone application fee is unchanged for everyone else.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to deny a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Fee Reduction Request of Steve McCleery for a Rezone Application from $1,350 to $760. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Subdivision plat rules amended

The council amended the city's rules for preliminary and final plats, the approval steps land must clear before it can be split into lots.

What it means: This changes the process for developers and anyone subdividing land; the motion does not spell out what the new rules require. Existing homeowners see no change.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 16.20 Preliminary Plats and 16.25 Final Plats. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Site plan rules changed, siding swaps left out

The council amended the city's site plan chapter but struck the proposed clause letting builders swap one approved exterior material for another.

What it means: Builders and their architects deal with this when filing plans; swapping siding materials still needs the usual review. Homeowners will not notice.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 17.100 Site Plans and omitting the proposed redline in 17.100.130 section “D” number nine (changing the exterior finishing materials from one acceptable material to another acceptable material as listen in the standards in the zone). Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Youth Council code adopted with mayor voting

With the mayor included this time, the council adopted the Youth Council code rewrite handing member appointments to the Youth Council Committee of Advisors.

What it means: The change now stands as city law. It reaches the teens who join the Youth Council and the staff who run it.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Repealing and Reenacting Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 2.45 Youth Council with changes as follows: • Include “Youth” Mayor when referring to the Youth Mayor and “Mayor” when referring to the appointed City Mayor, • In Section 2.45.040 Subsection E, committee approval by the City Manager will be replaced by Youth Council Committee of Advisors. • In Section 2.45.040 Subsection A.8., include “Elected City Mayor” when coordinating all activities with the Youth Council Committee of Advisors. • In 2.45.010 Section C: Change from “members are appointed to Youth Council by the City Manager” to “members are appointed by the Youth Council Committee of Advisors”. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

City to sell a small parcel to adjoining owners

The city approved selling 0.156 acres of city-owned land to the owners of the neighboring lots, with the sale to be finished by February 19, 2026.

What it means: Only the owners of the adjoining lots are affected. They can buy the parcels together on one application, and within six months their fences must sit on the correct lot line or be removed.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Disposal of 0.156 Acres of Designated "Significant Real Property" (Utah County Parcel #49:601:0080) Pursuant to EMMC 3.30.060 and 3.30.070, and Establishing Terms of the Disposal with terms below: • Approving option two with each landowner of the parcels listed signing the land disposal application. The applicants may combine the application into one land disposal application and fee. • If any landowner, in any of the lots are unwilling or unable to purchase the corresponding parcels, the approval will revert to option one. • It is required that all fences are on the correct lot line or the lots are to have no fence within 6 months of recordation. • The water requirements for each parcel are fulfilled with the purchase of the parcels as deemed appropriate by staff. • The full land disposal application and process must be finalized in six months (February 19, 2026). Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

RAP tax question goes to voters in November

The council put a 0.1% sales tax for recreation, arts, and cultural facilities on the November 4, 2025 ballot.

What it means: You decide this one at the polls. If it passes, you would pay an extra 10 cents on every $100 spent in the city, with the money going to recreation and cultural facilities.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Providing for a Referendum to be held on November 4, 2025, for the purpose of submitting to the qualified voters of Eagle Mountain City, Utah County, Utah the question of authorization of the imposition a 0.1% sales and use tax (one-tenth of one percent) for the purpose of funding recreational, cultural, botanical, and zoological facilities, and botanical, cultural, and zoological organizations within the City (RAP Tax). Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

Ask the state about archery at Rodeo Grounds

The council put archery shooting at the Rodeo Grounds on its list of priorities to raise with state lawmakers.

What it means: No archery range exists or is funded by this. It flags the idea for the city's state lobbying and nothing more.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to place archery shooting at the Rodeo Grounds to the Legislative Priorities List. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Youth Council vote retaken to include mayor

After the city attorney noted Mayor Westmoreland had been left out of the required vote, the council reopened the Youth Council code rewrite.

What it means: A procedural fix to a vote taken minutes earlier. It touches the record only.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes

Youth Council rules rewritten

The council rewrote the Youth Council chapter of city code, moving the power to appoint youth members from the city manager to a committee of advisors.

What it means: Families with a teen who wants to serve on the Youth Council now apply to that advisory committee. The rest of the city is untouched.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
  • Tom WestmorelandYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt Item 15.A., an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Repealing and Reenacting Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 2.45 Youth Council with changes as follows: • Include “Youth” Mayor when referring to the Youth Mayor and “Mayor” when referring to the appointed City Mayor, • In Section 2.45.040 Subsection E, committee approval by the City Manager will be replaced by Youth Council Committee of Advisors. • In Section 2.45.040 Subsection A.8., include “Elected City Mayor” when coordinating all activities with the Youth Council Committee of Advisors. • In 2.45.010 Section C: Change from “members are appointed to Youth Council by the City Manager” to “members are appointed by the Youth Council Committee of Advisors”. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Push state for veteran mortgage help

The council added veterans, active-duty military and public servants to the mortgage assistance program it will ask state lawmakers to support.

What it means: Nothing changes yet. This sets what the city lobbies the Legislature for, so any actual help with a down payment or mortgage would depend on state action later.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to add Veterans and Active-Duty Military Public Servants and Military to the Mortgage Assistance Program for the Legislative Priority List. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
