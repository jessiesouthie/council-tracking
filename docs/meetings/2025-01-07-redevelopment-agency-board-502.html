The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Wright moved to approve the Redevelopment Agency Board Meeting Minutes from December 3, 2024. Board Member Wood seconded the motion.

Deal to route Triple Tail tax money to the agency

The agency approved an agreement with Eagle Mountain City to collect and pass along the growth in property taxes from the Triple Tail project area.

What it means: This sets up the plumbing that moves tax money into the agency's project area account. Your own tax bill is unchanged, and you will not see anything on the ground.

passed with a unanimous vote.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Burnham moved to approve a Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between the Eagle Mountain Redevelopment Agency and Eagle Mountain City for the Collection and Remittance of Incremental Property Taxes Collected from Property Within the Triple Tail Community Reinvestment Project Area. Board Member Clark seconded the motion.
