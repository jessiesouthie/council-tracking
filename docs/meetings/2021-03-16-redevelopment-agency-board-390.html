Eagle Mountain Redevelopment Agency Board, 16 March 2021

6 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 6 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

June 2020 minutes approved

The agency board approved the written record of its June 16, 2020 meeting.

What it means: Signing off on old minutes is bookkeeping for the recorder's office. Nothing in it touches you.

Outcome not recorded

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Burnham moved to approve the June 16, 2020 minutes. Board Member Gray seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jared Gray, Carolyn Love, Colby Curtis, Melissa Clark, and Donna Burnham. The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Mayor Westmoreland named agency chair

The board named Tom Westmoreland chair of the redevelopment agency, the seat the mayor holds by default under agency bylaws adopted in 2008.

What it means: Chairing the agency lets the mayor speak for the city on economic development. Residents feel none of it.

passed with a unanimous vote.

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Gray moved to appoint Tom Westmoreland to serve as Chair of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City. Board Member Burnham seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jared Gray, Carolyn Love, Colby Curtis, Melissa Clark, and Donna Burnham. The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Carolyn Love named vice-chair

The agency board appointed Board Member Carolyn Love as vice-chair of the redevelopment agency for the year.

What it means: A vice-chair runs the meeting when the chair cannot. The choice stays inside the board and reaches no household.

Outcome not recorded

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Curtis moved to appoint Carolyn Love to serve as Vice-Chair of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City. Board Member Burnham seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jared Gray, Carolyn Love, Colby Curtis, Melissa Clark, and Donna Burnham- The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Plan adopted for Sweetwater data center area

The agency adopted the project area plan for Sweetwater Industrial Park reinvestment area #2, the framework for an agreement to bring a data center to the city.

What it means: Consultants told the board the project will not raise property taxes on other properties. The development agreement still has to go to the City Council, so the deal itself is not settled yet.

passed with a unanimous vote.

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Burnham moved to approve u resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, adopting an Official Project Area Plan for the Sweetwater Industrial Park Community Reinvestment Area #2. Board Member Gray seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jared Gray, Carolyn Love, Colby Curtis, Melissa Clark, and Donna Burnham The motion passed with a unanimous vote.

Sweetwater industrial park budget set at 67.5%

The agency adopted the budget for the Sweetwater Industrial Park reinvestment area #2, counting 67.5% of the site's real property tax value instead of the 75% first proposed.

What it means: Nobody's tax bill moves because of this. The percentage sets how much of the site's future property tax growth the agency puts back into the project rather than passing on to the county, schools, fire and water districts.

passed with a unanimous vote.

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Burnham moved to approve a resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, adopting an Official Project Area Budget for the Sweetwater Industrial Park Community Reinvestment Area #2, using the amended 67.5% real property tax value. Board Member Gray seconded the motion. Those voting aye: Jared Gray, Carolyn Love, Colby Curtis, Melissa Clark, and Donna Burnham. The motion passed with a unanimous vote.
