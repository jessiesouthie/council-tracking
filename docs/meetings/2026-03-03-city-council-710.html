Gas power plant approved on Pony Express Parkway

The city approved a development agreement with Will-Power UT for a natural gas power plant, limited to simple-cycle technology and required to be running within five years.

What it means: A gas-fired power plant is now cleared to be built, with trees required along Pony Express Parkway. If you live nearby, expect construction within the next five years unless the company gets an extension.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Craig WhitingYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
  • Zachory HuishYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a n Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah , approving the Development Agreement with Will -Power UT, LLC, conditioned on the following amendments to the agreement being made: 1. Clarify that only simple cycle natural gas technology may be used; and 2. Amend the additional property section to limit its use to the Stadion LLC parcel subject to the development agreement approved in October 2025; and 3. Amend the Timing and Rate of Development Section to require that the Generation Project be operational within five (5) years of the effective date of the agreement with the possibility of requesting an extension; and 4. That trees be planted along Pony Express Parkway in compliance with Eagle Mountain Municipal Code 16.35.080 . Councilmember Whiting seconded the motion.
