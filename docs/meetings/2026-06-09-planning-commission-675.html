Senior housing overlay zone recommended

The commission backed a new senior housing overlay zone, with accessory dwelling units barred inside it and five rural zones cut off from density bonuses.

What it means: If the council agrees, builders could add senior housing at higher density in some areas, though not in the RA1, RA2, RD1, RD2 or FR zones. Renting out a basement apartment would be off limits in these developments.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brent StrongYes
  • Bryan FreeYes
  • Chad DeCourseyYes
  • Jason AllenYes
  • Laura JensenExcused
  • Rod HessYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Hess moved to recommend approval to the City Council of Item 7.A., Senior Housing Overlay Zone with the following changes: 1. The Senior Housing Overlay Zone municipal code location shall be amended from Chapter 17.55 to Chapter 17.44; 2. 17.44.060(H): shall be amended to state, “Accessory Dwelling Units. Accessory dwelling units are prohibited within developments governed by the SHO unless permitted per Utah Code Title 10;” and 3. EMMC 17.25.030 Land Use Table: the RA1, RA2, RD1, RD2, and FR zones shall be marked with an asterisk and footnote, excluding them from qualifying for a density- incentive/bonus. Commissioner Allen seconded the motion.

Fee schedule sent to council with two fixes

The commission backed the city's consolidated fee schedule, asking that a 180-day deposit-holding line be struck and an unfinished footnote be completed.

What it means: The fee schedule sets what you pay the city for permits and inspections. Nothing changes yet: the council takes the final vote, and these two edits are cleanup rather than new charges.
