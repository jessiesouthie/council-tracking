Melissa Clark elected agency vice chair

The agency board elected Board Member Melissa Clark as its vice chairperson.

What it means: Filling a presiding-officer seat is internal housekeeping for the board. It touches who runs a meeting when the chair is out, and nothing else.

Abby Ivory named agency executive director

Abby Ivory, the city's new economic development director, was appointed executive director of the redevelopment agency by the agency board.

What it means: Ivory now runs the agency's daily work and speaks for it with other taxing entities. Staff shuffle only: your services and bills are untouched.

  • Melissa ClarkYes

The motion as the minutes record it
Board Member Wright moved to adopt a Resolution of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, Appointing Abby Ivory as Executive Director of the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City . Board Member Clark seconded the motion.
