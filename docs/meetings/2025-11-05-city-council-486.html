Eagle Mountain City Council, 5 November 2025

5 motions · 5 ordinance lines · 11 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

November named Hunger Awareness Month

The council proclaimed November 2025 as Hunger Awareness Month in Eagle Mountain.

What it means: This is a ceremonial statement — nothing changes for residents, and no money or city services are affected.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt a Proclamation of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Proclaiming November 2025 as Hunger Awareness Month. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Consent items approved, with school traffic conditions

The council approved its consent agenda and attached staff conditions requiring intersection improvements to the John Hancock Charter School west campus item.

What it means: Mostly routine housekeeping. The one piece with teeth is that the charter school's west campus must meet the city's conditions on intersection improvements, which matters to people driving near that site.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to approve the Consent Agenda and to also include the “Conditions of Approval on Intersection Improvements”, provided by Staff, for Item 12.A. for the John Hancock Charter School West Campus. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Sewer master plan and impact fee plan approved

The council approved the city's wastewater master plan and the impact fee facilities plan setting out which sewer projects new growth is expected to pay for.

What it means: Sewer service at existing homes doesn't change. The plan is the basis the city uses to charge impact fees on new construction, so it mainly touches builders and anyone building a new home here.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to approve a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Wastewater Master Plan and Impact Fee Facilities Plan (IFFP). Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Sweetwater #4 redevelopment plan adopted

The council adopted the Sweetwater #4 Community Reinvestment Area plan as the official plan governing redevelopment in that project area.

What it means: If you live or own property near Sweetwater #4, this sets the framework for what gets built there and how tax revenue from that growth is reinvested. The motion names no dollar amounts or timeline.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Adopting the Sweetwater #4 Community Reinvestment Area Project Area Plan, as Approved by the Redevelopment Agency of Eagle Mountain City, as the Official Community Reinvestment Project Area Plan for the Project Area and Directing that Notice of the Adoption be Given as Required by Statute. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Tax deal set up for Sweetwater #4 project area

The city and its redevelopment agency signed an agreement spelling out how property tax growth from the Sweetwater #4 project area gets collected and passed along.

What it means: Nothing changes on your tax bill. This is the paperwork that routes future tax growth from that one area to the redevelopment agency so the project plan can work.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to approve a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving an Interlocal Cooperation Agreement between the Eagle Mountain Redevelopment Agency and Eagle Mountain City, Utah for the Collection and Remittance of Incremental Property Taxes Collected from Property Within the Sweetwater #4 Community Reinvestment Project Area. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
