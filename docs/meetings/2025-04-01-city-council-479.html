What it means: No inland port district moves forward with the city's backing, so the industrial development and tax arrangements that come with one are off the table for now. Day to day, nothing changes.

What it means: This locks in how the planned sewer plant will clean wastewater and how it gets built. Nothing changes on your bill today — the motion names no cost or timeline.

passed with a unanimous vote.
