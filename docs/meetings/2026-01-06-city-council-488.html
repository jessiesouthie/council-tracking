Jason Allen seated on the Planning Commission

The council agreed to Jason Allen serving a three-year Planning Commission term, January 2026 through December 2028.

What it means: Residents who show up to speak on a development proposal will find him among the commissioners hearing it. No other effect.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Craig WhitingYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to consent to the appointment of Jason Allen for Planning Commission for a three-year term beginning January 2026 through December 2028. Councilmember Whiting seconded the motion.

Fence and screening rules changed

The city changed the part of its code that governs fences and screening, though the motion does not spell out the new provisions.

What it means: If you are putting up a fence or screening on your property anywhere in the city, check the current code before you build. The rules that apply to you have changed.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Craig WhitingYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 17.60.120 General Fencing/screening provisions. Councilmember Whiting seconded the motion.
