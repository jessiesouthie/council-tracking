Eagle Mountain City Council, 18 March 2025

7 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 9 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

March named MS Awareness Month

The council proclaimed March 2025 as Multiple Sclerosis Awareness Month in Eagle Mountain.

What it means: This is a ceremonial statement — nothing changes for residents, and no city money or rules are involved.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt a Proclamation of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Proclaiming March 2025 as Multiple Sclerosis (MS) Awareness Month. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Consent agenda approved, four items pulled

The council approved its consent agenda after pulling items 12.A, 12.B, 12.C and 13.C out for separate discussion.

What it means: Routine housekeeping — nothing changes for residents. The four pulled items were debated one at a time instead of being passed in a batch.

passed with a unanimous vote.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to approve the Consent Agenda removing items 12.A., 12.B., 12.C. and 13.C. from the agenda and moving them to the scheduled items. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Pinnacles B-3 site plan put on hold

The council set the master site plan for Pinnacles B-3 aside without deciding it.

What it means: Nothing is settled either way — the plan can come back to the council later. If you live near the Pinnacles area, this only delays the decision.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to table the Master Site Plan for Pinnacles B-3. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Harmony 13 subdivision plan denied

The council rejected the preliminary plat — the proposed lot layout — for the Harmony 13 subdivision.

What it means: As drawn, Harmony 13 does not move forward. That touches the developer and neighbors near the site; the motion itself gives no reasons for the denial.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayYes

  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to deny the Preliminary Plat, Harmony 13. Councilmember Gray seconded the motion.

The council considered rejecting the preliminary plat — the proposed lot layout — for the Harmony 14 subdivision.

What it means: This is the step where the city signs off on how a subdivision is laid out, so it affects the Harmony 14 developer and anyone living near the site. The record here gives no reasons.

Change to the Eagle Quest East development deal

The council approved the first amendment to the master development agreement covering the Eagle Quest East project.

What it means: The motion does not spell out what changed. A master development agreement sets the ground rules for how a project area gets built, so this matters mainly to the developer and to people living near Eagle Quest East.

passed with a 4:1 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt a Resolution Approving the First Amendment to the Eagle Quest East Master Development Agreement. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

ShopFest Utah agreement extended to 2035

The council took up an agreement with the Valley Crossroads Chamber of Commerce to run the ShopFest Utah event, lengthening the term from five years to ten, through 2035.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day. This sets the terms under which the chamber puts on ShopFest, and commits the city to that arrangement through 2035 rather than 2030.

(outcome not found)

  • Donna BurnhamYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving an Agreement with the Valley Crossroads Chamber of Commerce for the ShopFestTM Utah Event with the following changes: 1. In section five, changing the word “Chamber” to the word “City”, 2. In section ten, changing the term from five years to ten years, ending in 2035, 3. Add a “meet to confer” provision, 4. Change signature from Melissa Clark to Matt Everett, Chamber Treasurer, and 5. All future provisions for amendments to be in writing. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.
