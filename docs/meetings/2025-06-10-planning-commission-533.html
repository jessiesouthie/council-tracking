The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Hess moved to recommend approval to the City Council of 7. A. Athletic Facility Signs, with two recommendations. First, under the definition 17.80.160 to include: a sign attached to a fence surrounding and immediately adjacent to an athletic facility can be facing the interior or exterior of the athletic facility. Second, 17.80.120(R) to include: all signs must be attached to the fence, scoreboard, or bleachers and may not extend above the fence, scoreboard, or bleachers. Commissioner Allen seconded the motion, with the changes noted.
