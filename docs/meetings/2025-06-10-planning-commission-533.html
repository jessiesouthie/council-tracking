Eagle Mountain Planning Commission, 10 June 2025

5 motions · 4 ordinance lines · 1 resolution line

Motions and roll-call votes

New sign rules for athletic fields

The commission recommended sign rules for athletic facilities: signs must attach to the fence, scoreboard or bleachers, may face in or out, and may not rise above them.

What it means: Sports leagues and sponsors hanging banners at fields would follow these limits once the council acts. People living near a field could see signs facing outward.

(outcome not found)

  • Jason AllenYes
  • Robert FoxYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Commissioner Hess moved to recommend approval to the City Council of 7. A. Athletic Facility Signs, with two recommendations. First, under the definition 17.80.160 to include: a sign attached to a fence surrounding and immediately adjacent to an athletic facility can be facing the interior or exterior of the athletic facility. Second, 17.80.120(R) to include: all signs must be attached to the fence, scoreboard, or bleachers and may not extend above the fence, scoreboard, or bleachers. Commissioner Allen seconded the motion, with the changes noted.

Allow 1 to 2 vehicles or RVs owned by others on a property

The commission recommended approval of Item 7B, adding that 1 to 2 vehicles or RVs not titled to the owner may be parked on the property if all other requirements are met.

What it means: This could matter if you park a friend's or relative's RV or car on land covered by this item. It is not in force: the City Council decides.

Transportation master plan pushed to June 24

The commission delayed a decision on the Transportation Master Plan and its Impact Fee Facilities Plan, and kept the public hearing open until June 24, 2025.

What it means: Nothing about roads or fees changed at this meeting. If you want to speak on the plan, the hearing is still open on June 24.
