Joint Work Session — Regional Transportation

Eagle Mountain City Council, 8 January 2025

0 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 0 resolution lines

Summary

Multi-city work session at Fox Hollow Golf Course (American Fork) with Cedar Fort, Fairfield, Lehi, Saratoga Springs, UTA, and UDOT. UDOT and Hales Engineering presented the Point-of-the-Mountain and north Utah Lake transportation focus areas, projecting ~670,000 buildout population and proposing a collector-distributor system on I-15. Discussion centered on growth-projection accuracy, EIS coordination through MAG, and the need for a unified priority list and timeline. Minutes provided by American Fork City.
