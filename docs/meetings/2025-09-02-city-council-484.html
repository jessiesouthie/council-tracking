All meetings / 2 September 2025

Eagle Mountain City Council, 2 September 2025

6 motions · 10 ordinance lines · 5 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Susan Allen named senior citizen advisor

Susan Allen was appointed as a Senior Citizen Advisor, with the term running through December 31, 2025.

What it means: One advisory seat, filled for the rest of the year. Your taxes, your property and your city services are untouched by it.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayExcused
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to approve the appointment of Susan Allen as a Senior Citizen Advisor through December 31, 2025. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Consent agenda approved

The council approved its consent agenda in a single vote; the record here does not list which items were on it.

What it means: Consent items are the ones the council has already agreed are uncontroversial enough to pass together. Without a list of what was included, there is nothing here a resident can act on.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayExcused
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to approve the Consent Agenda. Councilmember Burnham seconded the motion.

Land rezoned for the new elementary school

The council rezoned the land for Alpine School District's Elementary 133 so the school can be built there.

What it means: Paired with the site plan approval, this lets the school district move ahead. Neighbors will get a school on that land instead of whatever the previous zoning allowed.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayExcused
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved to adopt Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Rezoning Certain Lands Known as Alpine School District Elementary 133. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

New Alpine School District elementary school approved

The council approved the preliminary plat and site plan for Alpine School District's Elementary 133.

What it means: If you live near the site, expect school construction and eventually the traffic a new elementary school brings. This approval clears the way for the district to build.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved adopt Alpine School District Elementary 133 Preliminary Plat and Site Plan. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Water master plan and impact fee study adopted

The council adopted the city's long-range plan for drinking water, along with the impact fee facilities plan that goes with it.

What it means: Builders pay attention here: the plan is the engineering basis the city uses to justify water impact fees on new construction. If you already have a house and a water meter, nothing about your service changes today.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayExcused
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah approving the Culinary Water Master Plan and Impact Fee Facilities Plan (IFFP). Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Changes to the Ault Farms (Parkway Fields) development deal

The council approved the first amendment to the Ault Farms (Parkway Fields) master development agreement, adding a nine-month sunset clause to the amendment.

What it means: This touches the Parkway Fields area rather than the city at large, and the amended terms expire after nine months. The motion itself does not say what else in the agreement changed.

passed with a unanimous 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayExcused
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wood moved adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah Approving the First Amendment to Master Development Agreement for Ault Farms (Parkway Fields), including a nine-month sunset clause in the Amendment. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.
