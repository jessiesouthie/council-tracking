The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to deny an ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code, Section 17.60.160 Zone Transitions. Councilmember Curtis seconded the motion. Councilmember Wright stated that he moved to deny the item because his concerns have not been resolved. He might be open to reconsidering the amendment at a future date but was uncomfortable with approving it in conjunction with an application. However, his sense is that other members of the Council are not in favor of approval and he desires to move forward to consider other items.
