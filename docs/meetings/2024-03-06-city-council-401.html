Lot line changes allowed by survey, not a plat

The council changed city code so a property line between a subdivision and an outlying parcel can be adjusted by survey instead of a plat amendment.

What it means: Shifting a boundary with a neighboring parcel now takes a survey rather than a full plat amendment. Staff will also record a zoning notice on the property, so future buyers can see when land sits in more than one zone.
