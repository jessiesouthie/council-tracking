All meetings / 27 October 2020

Planning Commission Regular Session

Eagle Mountain Planning Commission, 27 October 2020

5:30 PM

The city published no minutes for this meeting. What follows is the agenda it posted beforehand, so it says what the council intended to take up — not what was decided, and not who voted for it. If minutes are approved later, they will replace this page.

What was on the agenda

1. Discussion Items

  • 1.A Discussion Item Proposed Code Amendment to Change EMMC 17.58 Historical Preservation Zone to Historical Preservation Overlay Zone A discussion on proposed changes to EMMC 17.58 Historical Preservation Zone to change the zoning tool to an overlay zone and add clarity to the designation process.
  • 1.B Discussion Item Proposed Code Amendment to Revise EMMC 17.62 Ridgeline Protection Standards to Ridgeline Protection Overlay Zone A discussion on proposed changes to EMMC 17.62 Ridgeline Protection Standards in order to establish an overlay zone under the current regulations.
  • 1.C Discussion Item Proposed Code Amendment to Remove Lot Size Transitioning from EMMC 17.60 A discussion about the proposed code amendment to remove the lot size transition section (and related references to it) within EMMC 17.60 Landscaping, Buffering, Fencing and Transitioning. The recent adoption of the zoning transition section to this chapter achieves the same purpose and intent. 6:30 P.M. - Eagle Mountain City Planning Commission Policy Session

2. Pledge of Allegiance

3. Declaration of Conflicts of Interest

4. Approval of Meeting Minutes

  • 4.A October 13, 2020, Planning Commission Minutes

5. Status Report

6. Action and Advisory Items

  • 6.A Pony Express Townhomes (Concept Plan) Concept plan review for the next phase of Pony Express Townhomes located on approximately 4.6 acres. Access to this portion of the development would be off of N Sun Crest Drive in the New Park at Porter's Crossing subdivision. The concept plan includes 27 townhome units, with nine buildings of three units each.
  • 6.B Scenic Mountain Phase C (Concept Plan) A concept plan for Phase C of the Scenic Mountain project. The Scenic Mountain project includes an approved Master Development Agreement (MDA). Portions of Phases A and B have been built or are under construction. The applicant is looking to develop the plans for Phase C based on the and is seeking feedback on the concept plan before submitting their next set of applications.
  • 6.C Oquirrh Mountain Ranch Phase B (Concept Plan) A concept plan for Phase B of the Oquirrh Mountain Ranch project. The Oquirrh Mountain Ranch project includes an approved Master Development Agreement. A majority of Phase A has been built. The applicant is considering an amendment request to the Master Development Agreement with regards to Phase B and is seeking feedback on the concept plan.
  • 6.D Public Hearing Development Code Amendment to Remove Tier and Bonus Density Language (EMMC Titles 7, 16, and 17) This is a request to amend the municipal development code for EMMC Title 7 Parks, Trails and Special Events; Title 16 Subdivisions; and Title 17 Zoning in order to remove references to "tier" and "base/bonus density." These phrases related to EMMC 17.30 Residential Zone Bonus Density Entitlements which was repealed by ordinance of the City Council in 2019.
  • 6.E Development Code Amendment for Fencing Standards (EMMC 16.35 & 17.60) This is a request to amend the municipal development code for EMMC Chapter 16.35 Development Standards for Required Public Facilities and Chapter 17.60 Landscaping, Buffering, Fencing and Transitioning. The amendment includes revisions to clarify fencing standards and requirements. The Planning Commission held a public hearing on this request on October 13, 2020. The public hearing was opened and closed at that time. The request was tabled for minor edits to the code amendment.
