Marcia Vasquez appointed to library board

Marcia Vasquez was appointed a Library Board advisor for a three-year term through December 2028.

What it means: The board advises on how the library runs. Library hours, cards and services are unchanged by the appointment itself.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Mandy Lane appointed planning commissioner

Mandy Lane was appointed to the Planning Commission for a one-year term through December 2026.

What it means: The Planning Commission reviews zoning and development proposals before they reach the council. Her seat comes up again at the end of the year.

Chad Decoursey joins planning commission as alternate

Chad Decoursey was appointed a Planning Commission alternate for a three-year term running through December 2028.

Lacie Messerly named city recorder

Lacie Messerly was appointed as Eagle Mountain City Recorder.

Youth Council rules rewritten

The council rewrote the section of city code that sets up and governs the Youth Council, with revisions worked out earlier the same night.

What it means: This touches only the teens who serve on the Youth Council and those applying to join. For everyone else it is internal housekeeping.

Amenity swap rules added to city's law wish list

The council put a new rule on its legislative priorities list requiring developers to raise amenity exchange requests early in the approval process.

What it means: Nothing changes yet: this only flags the idea for a future code change. Developers swapping one promised amenity for another would eventually have to ask sooner.
