Eagle Mountain City Council, 2 January 2024

3 motions · 2 ordinance lines · 3 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Consent items changed: trees, $40,000 for parks

Approval of the consent agenda came with conditions: trees or their cash value from Stadion LLC, a $40,000 park donation from Comcast, and a revised meeting calendar.

What it means: The tree and park money comes out of development deals, not your utility bill, so nothing changes at home. If you attend meetings, the March 5 date is gone and March 6 and November 6 were added.

passed with a unanimous vote.

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

Third change to this year's budget

For the third time this budget year, the council changed the city's 2023-2024 spending plan, though the motion lists no dollar amounts.

What it means: What moved, and by how much, is not stated in the record of this vote. You would need the amendment document itself to see which accounts changed.

New city website, $35,000 contract

The city hired Web Development Group Platforms to build a new city website for $35,000, leaving staff to settle a payment schedule.

What it means: Anyone who uses the city site to find an agenda, a form or a meeting date will eventually land on a rebuilt version. No launch date was set.
