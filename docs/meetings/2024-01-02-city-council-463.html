Consent items changed: trees, $40,000 for parks

Approval of the consent agenda came with conditions: trees or their cash value from Stadion LLC, a $40,000 park donation from Comcast, and a revised meeting calendar.

What it means: The tree and park money comes out of development deals, not your utility bill, so nothing changes at home. If you attend meetings, the March 5 date is gone and March 6 and November 6 were added.
