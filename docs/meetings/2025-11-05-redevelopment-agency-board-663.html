Plan adopted for the Sweetwater #4 project area

The board adopted the official plan for the Sweetwater #4 community reinvestment project area, the document setting out what the agency intends to do there.

City sends its Sweetwater #4 tax growth to agency

Eagle Mountain City itself will send its property tax growth from the Sweetwater #4 area to the agency, in an agreement the board approved.

What it means: The city and the agency are separate legal entities, so the city has to sign to hand over its own increment. Homeowners see nothing different on a bill.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Board Member Gray seconded the motion.

Utah County agrees to Sweetwater #4 tax split

The board signed the county into the arrangement too: property tax growth Utah County collects inside Sweetwater #4 goes to the agency.

What it means: Property tax the county collects inside the project area funds work in that area. If you live elsewhere in the city, this one passes you by.

passed with a unanimous vote.

Water district to route Sweetwater #4 tax growth

Central Utah Water Conservancy District will hand its share of that same property tax growth to the agency, an agreement the board put in place.

What it means: Each taxing entity has to sign separately before the agency can use the money, and this is one of those signatures. You will not notice it anywhere.

Fire district signs on to Sweetwater #4 tax deal

The agency board approved an agreement with the Unified Fire Service Area to collect and pass along property tax growth from the Sweetwater #4 project area.

What it means: New property tax raised inside that one area goes to the agency rather than to the fire district. No tax rate is set by this, and no bill of yours changes.
