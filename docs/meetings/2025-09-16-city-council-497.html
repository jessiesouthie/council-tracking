Eagle Mountain City Council, 16 September 2025

7 motions · 7 ordinance lines · 10 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Consent agenda approved, one item pulled out

The council approved its bundle of routine consent items but pulled the Mountainland Supply development agreement out for separate discussion.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this is the batch of routine business the council passes in a single vote, minus the one item it wanted to debate on its own.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to approve the Consent Agenda removing item 14.A. to be placed on the Scheduled Items for further discussion. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Development deal with Mountainland Supply approved

The city approved a development agreement with Mountainland Supply; the motion itself does not state the site, the size, or the terms.

What it means: A development agreement locks in what a builder may do on a specific piece of land. The motion doesn't say where that land is or what is going up, so the details sit in the agreement itself.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving a Development Agreement with Mountainland Supply. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Lower $45,000-an-acre price rejected

The council rejected a lower price of $45,000 per acre ($1.03 per square foot) for the same city land, on the argument that the parcel needs no added water rights.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this was a competing price for the same parcel, and the higher price the council approved is what stands.

failed for lack of a majority vote, 1:4.

  • Brett WrightNo
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayNo
  • Melissa ClarkNo
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing the Disposal of Significant City-Owned Real Property Pursuant to Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 3.30 and using the price of the land at $45,000 per acre ($1.03 per square foot) nothing the parcel does not require additional water rights. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Bid to pause the land sale over water rules fails

The council declined to delay the land-sale resolution while staff refined water requirements and wrote a policy for reviewing future requests to buy city land.

What it means: Nothing changes for residents — this was an attempt to hold the sale back for more study, and it did not hold.

failed for lack of a majority vote, 2:3.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamNo
  • Jared GrayNo
  • Melissa ClarkNo
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to table a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing the Disposal of Significant City-Owned Real Property Pursuant to Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 3.30 and directed Staff to work on refining water requirements and draft a policy to ensure consistent review of future Land Disposal Applications. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

City land to be sold at $95,000 an acre

The council authorized selling a significant piece of city-owned property at a price of $95,000 per acre, or $2.18 per square foot.

What it means: The motion doesn't name the parcel or the buyer; what it settles is the price the city will accept for land the public currently owns. Nothing changes day to day for residents.

passed with a 3:2 vote.

  • Brett WrightNo
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Gray moved to adopt a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing the Disposal of Significant City-Owned Real Property Pursuant to Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Chapter 3.30 with a sales price of $95,000 per acre ($2.18 per square foot). Councilmember Clark seconded the motion.

Telecom rules added to council's priority list

The council added drafting a telecommunications ordinance and/or franchise agreement to its list of legislative priorities.

What it means: Nothing changes for you yet — this puts the item on the city's to-do list, and any actual rules would have to come back to the council later.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to add drafting a Telecommunications Ordinance and/or Franchise Agreement to the Legislative Priorities List. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Land-sale policy added to council's priority list

The council put writing a policy for how staff review applications to buy city-owned land onto its list of legislative priorities.

What it means: Nothing changes right now — this only commits the city to drafting rules later for how it handles requests to buy public land.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes

  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes

The motion as the minutes record it
