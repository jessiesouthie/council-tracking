Community Services Board Meeting

Eagle Mountain Community Services Board, 20 May 2026

0 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 0 resolution lines

Summary

The board reviewed the Memorial Day program and adopted a revised governance article (Rules of Order & Civil Discourse) to recommend to the City Council. It discussed board expectations and a rec-sports sponsorship policy — passing a motion to accept jersey sponsorships at $2,500 (front), $2,500 (back), and $1,000 (sleeve) for one full year, aimed at covering jersey costs; Jessie Southworth will draft the policy. Charlotte Ducos presented an America 250 / EM30 'passport' concept tying anniversary events to resident prizes. On electric dirt bikes / OHVs, the board gave staff direction to prohibit motor vehicles on paved trails, set an ~15 mph limit with signage, and tighten code definitions, with a proposal to go to the City Council. The board also planned a Pony Express Days Family Fun Night input booth, began outlining overall priorities (Parks, Trails, Arts, Recreation) and RAP Tax planning (deferred), and directed staff to apply for a ~$25,000 Municipal Recreation Grant (due June 8) focused on trail connections. Prior minutes were approved; adjourned at 9:02 PM.
