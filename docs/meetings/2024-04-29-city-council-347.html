3 motions · 0 ordinance lines · 2 resolution lines

School district agreement restated a third time

The council ran the same three-city school district agreement through a third resolution, again acknowledging the central district being formed by Alpine, Highland, Cedar Hills, Lehi and American Fork.

What it means: Duplicate paperwork on a decision that matters: the schools your children attend are what is at stake, but this particular item adds nothing to it.

New school district with Saratoga Springs and Fairfield

The council took up an agreement with Saratoga Springs and Fairfield Town to create their own school district, and backed five neighboring cities forming a central district.

What it means: If you have school-age children, this points toward a district shared with Saratoga Springs and Fairfield rather than the one your schools sit in now. What changes in classrooms and on tax bills was not settled here.
