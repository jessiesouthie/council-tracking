Consent agenda approved, one item moved for discussion

The council cleared its consent agenda, holding three items back for separate votes, shifting Item 13.A. to the discussion portion of the meeting, and correcting spelling in Item 13.B.

Longer construction hours OK'd for QTS data center

The city let the QTS SLC1 data center site run construction outside the hours normally allowed under city code.

What it means: If you live near the QTS SLC1 site, construction noise may continue outside the usual permitted hours. The motion does not say which hours were granted or how long the exception lasts.

passed with a 4:0 vote.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to approve a Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Authorizing Extended Hours for Construction Operations for QTS – SLC1 as Allowed in Eagle Mountain Municipal Code Section 8.15.080(G). Councilmember Huish seconded the motion.

Overland development deal amended, lights capped at 25 feet

The city approved a change to the Overland development agreement, requiring 25-foot light poles instead of 30-foot and a finished 35-acre land swap with the state.

What it means: If you live near the Overland project, outdoor lighting will sit five feet lower than planned, which means less glare. The deal also depends on a state land exchange tied to the planned bypass road.

passed with a 4:0 vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Craig WhitingYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodExcused
  • Zachory HuishYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Whiting moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain, Utah, Approving the 5 th Amendment to the Amended and Restated Overland Master Development Agreement conditioned on the following: 1. Lighting fixtures shall be 25 feet in height instead of 30 feet; and 2. Completion of the SITLA (State School and Institutional Trust Lands Administration) Land Exchange A greement and all agreements related to the bypass road 35 -acre land exchange with SITLA . Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.
