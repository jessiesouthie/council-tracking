Eagle Mountain City Council, 4 March 2025

4 motions · 3 ordinance lines · 12 resolution lines

Motions and roll-call votes

Consent agenda approved, two items moved

The council approved its consent agenda and pulled the downtown plan and inland port resolutions out for separate debate later in the meeting.

What it means: This one rearranged the evening's running order. Both pulled items got their own discussion; no resident is touched by the shuffle itself.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to approve the Consent Agenda, removing items 12.C. and 12.D. from the agenda and placing them after Item 13.A. on the regular agenda. Councilmember Rich Wood seconded the motion.

Enyo development agreement taken up

The council considered an ordinance approving a development agreement with Enyo, as written in the meeting's agenda packet.

What it means: A development agreement sets what a developer may build and what the city requires in return. The motion itself gives no location, size, or dollar figures, so the terms are only in the packet.

Outcome not recorded

No roll call was recorded for this motion.

The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Wright moved to adopt an Ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, Approving the Enyo Development Agreement as presented in the agenda packet. Councilmember Wood seconded the motion.

Downtown Master Plan adopted

The council adopted the Downtown Master Plan, the city's guiding document for how its downtown area develops.

What it means: The plan shapes what gets built downtown in the years ahead, so builders and property owners there will work from it. The motion names no specific project, acreage, or cost.

passed with a vote of 3:2.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayNo
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodNo
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to adopt the Resolution of Eagle Mountain City, Utah Adopting the Downtown Master Plan. Councilmember Wright seconded the motion.

Inland port proposal put on hold

The council set aside a resolution backing a Utah Inland Port Authority project area in Eagle Mountain, holding it for more discussion.

What it means: Nothing is settled about an inland port here. If the idea concerns or interests you, the debate is still open and will come back to the council.

passed with a unanimous vote.

  • Brett WrightYes
  • Donna BurnhamYes
  • Jared GrayYes
  • Melissa ClarkYes
  • Rich WoodYes
The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Clark moved to table the Resolution, Supporting the Creation of a Utah Inland Port Authority Project Area in Eagle Mountain City, for further discussion. Councilmember Rich Wood seconded the motion.
