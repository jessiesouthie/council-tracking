Triple Tail project area plan adopted again

On a second vote, the agency adopted the official plan for the Triple Tail Community Reinvestment Project Area, this time with no acreage change attached.

What it means: This is the version of the plan that stands. Landowners and developers inside the Triple Tail area deal with it; the record says nothing that changes for people living outside it.

Triple Tail project area budget adopted

The agency adopted the spending plan for the Triple Tail reinvestment area, correcting the area's size from 671.49 acres to 1,170.44 acres.

What it means: The corrected figure covers about 499 more acres than the resolution first said. It matters to landowners and builders inside that area; if you live elsewhere in the city, this one passes you by.
