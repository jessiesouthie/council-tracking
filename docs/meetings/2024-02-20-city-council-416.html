Rodeo and demolition derby join Pony Express Days

The council formally recognized the PRCA rodeo and the demolition derby as official events of the city's Pony Express Days celebration.

What it means: Nothing changes day to day for residents — this is a formal blessing that lets the rodeo and derby run under the city's Pony Express Days banner.
