The motion as the minutes record it
Councilmember Burnham moved to table an ordinance of Eagle Mountain City, Utah, amending the Eagle Mountain Municipal Code, Chapter 17.80 Sign Regulations and Sign Permits. Councilmember Clark seconded the motion. Councilmember Gricius stated the updated code should include approval for inflatable signs, and stated she would approve the amendment with a permit required, limited to fifteen consecutive days, and allowing two per business. She stated her preference of four times ayear, versus two per year. She said twelve consecutive days would allow a full business week with two weekends. Councilmember Clark stated the time limit for enforcement should be updated, the permitted days should be consecutive, and signs should be limited to posting twice a year. She stated businesses would be able to use two of the four allowed periods together and have a sign up for thirty days. The Council stated they understand that is a possibility. Councilmember Reaves agteed with previous comments from the Council. He said businesses use different ways to advertise and he likes the inflatable signs, as long as they are regulated. He stated fifteen days may be too long, but businesses should be allowed to post signs four times a year. Councilmember Burnham stated she prefers to allow the signs up to four times a year. She also likes Mr. Cook's suggestions regarding changing the enforcement timing. Councilmember Curtis said he would prefer ten days, instead of fifteen days, and expressed concern that allowing these types of signs will cause visual pollution. Ms. West asked if the ordinance can be placed under special event signs. Councilmember Gricius stated the special event signs section of the code can be amended to include the restrictions discussed at this meeting. Discussion ensued regarding special event sign restrictions. Those voting aye: Colby Curtis, Stephanie Gricius, Benjamin Reaves, Donna Burnham, and Melissa Clark. The motion passed with a unanitnous vote" 19, ORDINANCE 19.
