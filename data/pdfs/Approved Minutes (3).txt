8:30 AM ANNUAL VISIONING RETREAT    -  DAY 2 
 
1. WELCOME    
 
2. DISCUSSION AND INFORMATION ITEMS       
 
      2.A . Welcome from Mayor Gray  
 
Mayor Gray called  the second day of the retreat to order at 8: 30  AM. He delivered a 
welcome address  to Council and Staff in attendance.  
 
      2.B . Recreation Programs, Facilities, and Events   
 
Jason Hall presented the allocation of Eagle Mountain Recreation programs, including the 
event and priority list.  
 
Events Coordinator Dawn Hancock presented options for the parade route . Public Safety 
Director David Ulibarri provided input regarding public safety considerations for the route . 
 
A break was held from 10:10 AM to 10:17 AM, after which the retreat resumed with Deputy 
City Manager Steve Mumford presenting the City Growth Report, which was on the agenda 
for day one but was not discussed at that time.  
 
      2.C.  Strategic Planning Training and Discussion

Director of Legislative and Strategic Affairs Evan Berrett presented Eagle Mountain’s 
Strategic Plan and provided an overview of this year’s Utah Legislative Session  (which was 
scheduled as item 2.G ). 
 
A lunch break was held from 12:07 P M to 12:26 P M. 
 
      2.D.  Open Mayor and Council Discussion  
 
Mayor Gray resumed the meeting to discuss operational efficiencies and City structure 
moving forward. Councilmembers and staff explored ways to streamline workflows and 
improve collaboration.  
 
      2.E.  City Facilities Deep Dive  
 
City Manager Ben Reeves opened the discussion to Council and Staff regarding options for 
Eagle Mountain facilities . 
 
A lunch break was held from 2:35 PM to 2:48 PM. 
 
      2.F.  Team Building Activity  
 
Assistant to the City Manager Natalie Winterton presented Time Capsule options and ideas.  
 
      2.G.  Utah Legislative Session Discussion  
 
      2.H.  Moving Forward Together  
 
At the conclusion of the retreat, City Manager Ben Reeves and the Council discussed 
reorganization of the Boards and Committees list. Mayor Gray led discussions on options 
and updates for the Future Use Lane Map. He also facilitated discussion regarding the roles 
and responsibilities of the Mayor and City Council.  
 
3. ADJOURNMENT     
 
The second and final day of the retreat concluded at 6:45  PM.
